# CAP-001 — Turn Quick Capture into an actionable inbox

Status: experimental triage inbox.

## Before change

Example: a fleeting note was mainly text plus timestamps; after capture, triage required conversion or removal without priority, due date, project, or explicit inbox state.

## After change

Fleeting notes preserve source, priority, tags, due date, project, and triage state. Capture cards expose in-progress, archive, priority, and conversion actions while keeping the original until explicit removal. The main/preload bridge supports metadata updates.

## Verification

Fleeting-note normalization/conversion tests pass. Global shortcut/clipboard capture and batch triage are still optional extensions.

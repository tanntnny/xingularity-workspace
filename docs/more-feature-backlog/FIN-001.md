# FIN-001 — Connect subscriptions to real financial and calendar decisions

Status: experimental renewal/payment context.

## Before change

Example: a subscription stored renewal and billing data but could not retain payment history, reminder lead times, cancellation contact, usage-review state, or invoice attachment references.

## After change

Subscription records and IPC accept renewal reminder days, cancellation URL/contact, usage-review state, payment history, currency provenance, and attachment references. The subscriptions drawer edits the new fields and payment history is stored with the record.

## Verification

Subscription normalization/service tests pass. Provider/bank imports and currency-rate fetching remain deliberately manual/local.
